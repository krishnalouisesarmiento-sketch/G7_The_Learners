Solution

A camera is an input device that captures visual information and converts physical images into digital data that computers can process, store, and share. It solves problems in ICT by bridging the gap between the physical world and digital systems, allowing technology to "see" and interpret information. It enables automation, real-time monitoring, and accurate visual communication, while supporting advanced technologies like artificial intelligence to make processes faster, safer, and more efficient.

  1. Hold The Camera: Grip the sides of the camera firmly with both hands to keep it steady while using.
  2. How to Zoom: Locate the rotating ring around the lens. Turn the ring clockwise or counter-clockwise to adjust the zoom level and get closer or further away from your subject.
  3. How to Take a Picture: Look through the front lens or viewfinder to frame your shot. Once you are ready, press the shutter button located on the top of the camera to capture the image.

Result

The survey shows that the majority of respondents are photographers who identify as enthusiasts and are experienced in using cameras, indicating that the target users are knowledgeable and skilled.

Users gave positive feedback regarding the visual design and ease of use, believing the camera will effectively meet their needs, though they suggested improvements such as adding a rubber grip, making buttons clearer, and enhancing the interface to make the design more unique and user-friendly.
